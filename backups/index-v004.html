Resolution & Storage

Any square size works - try 32, 64, 96, 128, 160... higher resolutions train slower (roughly with the square of the size) but may resolve finer detail; lower resolutions are much faster to iterate with, especially for the SNN.

Storage: in-browser (IndexedDB) - use Export to get real files.
